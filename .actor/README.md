# LeadGap AI — Local Business Opportunity Finder

Find and rank qualified local-business opportunities for web design, SEO, automation, and digital agencies.

LeadGap analyzes local businesses and helps identify where there is a real service opportunity — for example, a missing website, weak online presence, poor conversion setup, or other visible digital gaps.

## What it does

- finds local businesses in your target market
- analyzes their current web presence
- identifies actionable business gaps
- scores and ranks opportunities
- returns contact and business data when available
- suggests relevant services you could pitch
- generates outreach hooks for qualified leads

## Best for

- web design agencies
- SEO agencies
- automation agencies
- freelancers and consultants
- local lead-generation teams
- outbound sales workflows

## Output

Each qualified opportunity can include:

- business name
- category
- location
- website
- phone
- email when available
- opportunity score
- detected gaps
- recommended services
- personalized outreach hook

## Pricing

Pricing starts from $24 per 1,000 qualified opportunities.

## How to use

1. Choose your target location and business category.
2. Configure the search criteria.
3. Run LeadGap.
4. Review the ranked opportunities.
5. Export the results for outreach or CRM use.

## Notes

LeadGap is designed to surface qualified opportunities, not just raw business listings. Results depend on the availability and quality of public business data.
